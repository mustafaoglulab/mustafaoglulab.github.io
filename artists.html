Lab Artists

Celebrating the multidisciplinary dialogue between art, biology, and technology.

The Mustafaoglu Lab actively collaborates with Gate 27, an international residency program that facilitates dialogue between art, science, and social impact. By opening our biology laboratories at Sabancı University, we host artists to explore biotechnology, cellular structures, and sustainable biomaterials. Through these week-long residencies, artists and researchers co-create at the interface of science and art.

2023 Resident

Esin Avcı Aykanat

April - May 2023 Gate 27 Program

During her residency, Esin Avcı Aykanat collaborated with Dr. Nur Mustafaoğlu and her research team at Sabancı University to explore biological decellularization techniques. Stripping chlorophyll and other organic matter from plant leaves, she generated translucent cellulose scaffolds that mirror the human vascular network. Her project investigates the boundaries of life and death, human-nature linkages, and serves as a biological archive exploring the potential for cellular re-seeding and future revival.

2024 Resident

Damla Yalçın

May 2024 ORTA Bluesky Program

As the inaugural artist for the ORTA Bluesky residency program (a collaboration between Gate 27 and ORTA Anadolu), Damla Yalçın researched alternative biomaterials in the Mustafaoglu Lab. Focusing on growing and cultivating SCOBY (Symbiotic Culture of Bacteria and Yeast) sheets, she integrated these bacterial cellulose layers with denim fabrics for her installation "Living Spaces." Her work probes the future of textiles, illustrating how living organisms can be co-opted into fabric fabrication for sustainable manufacturing.
