Standard Data Collection Codebook & Guidelines

Handbook Section 4 & 5: Standard definitions, outcome classifications, and distinction between documented findings and possible explanations.

Outcome Classifications (Section 4.3)

Positive outcome

The prespecified primary endpoint was met, according to reported evidence and statistical analysis plan.

Negative outcome

The primary endpoint was not met, or lack of efficacy was documented in the final published trial results.

Mixed / Inconclusive

Mixed results (e.g. secondary endpoints showed trends but primary endpoint missed), uncertain findings, or conflicting evidence.

Terminated / Discontinued

Study stopped early prior to target enrollment. The documented reason (e.g. futility, toxicity, sponsor decision) must be recorded.

Not assessable

Insufficient publicly accessible outcome information. Do not guess; record available details.

Documented Reasons vs Contributing Factors (Section 4.4)

Crucial Scientific Rule: Students must strictly distinguish between findings explicitly reported in the trial vs hypothesized translational factors!

1. Documented Reasons

Findings explicitly reported in ClinicalTrials.gov results, peer-reviewed publications, or FDA/EMA regulatory briefing documents.

  • Primary endpoint (e.g., ADAS-Cog, mRS, OS) p > 0.05 vs placebo.
  • Futility threshold crossed during planned interim analysis.
  • Excess adverse events: intolerable cerebral edema, ARIA-E, hemorrhage.

2. Possible Contributing Factors

Mechanisms suggested by scientific literature and translational neuropharmacology. Must cite supporting evidence.

  • Blood-Brain Barrier (BBB) delivery: Low brain bioavailability (<0.1–0.2% plasma concentration reaching CNS parenchymal targets).
  • Disease Heterogeneity: Subtypes (e.g., IDH-wildtype vs mutant GBM; ischemic penumbra variations).
  • Therapeutic Window & Patient Timing: Administering intervention after irreversible neuronal death.

Study Selection Criteria (Section 4.2)

Criterion Preferred Selection Disease-Specific Considerations
Study Type Interventional Exclude purely observational or imaging-only registries.
Phase Phase II or Phase III Phase IIb or Phase II/III seamless trials are acceptable.
Status Completed or Terminated Active or recruiting trials should be excluded from outcome analysis.
Intervention Drug or Biological Small molecules, monoclonal antibodies, gene/cell therapy.
Period 2010 – 2026 Studies completed in this window reflect contemporary trial design.
Disease Nuances Specific clinical scope AD: Specify early vs mild-to-moderate.
GBM: Distinguish newly diagnosed from recurrent.
Stroke: Focus on acute ischemic treatment.

Quality Control 6-Step Verification Protocol (Section 5)

1
Verify NCT ID & Relevance: Confirm registration link on ClinicalTrials.gov and target brain disease.
2
Check Design & Regimen: Confirm phase, randomization, blinding, and active vs placebo control.
3
Confirm Reported Outcome: Check primary endpoint result in publication abstract, results table, or press release.
4
Trace Supporting Evidence: Record valid DOI and PMID references.
5
Independent Peer Check: Second teammate reviews and marks corrections.
6
Resolve Disagreements: Document changes in QC Log and confirm final classification.
